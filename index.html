About

I would rather make the boring part solid than hide it behind polish.

My background is a mix of development, support, and fixing things that are already in use. That has made me fairly practical: before I reach for a clever solution, I want to understand what is actually failing and who it affects.

Backend development appeals to me because the work is measurable. The code either handles the case correctly, stores the data safely, responds quickly enough, and gives the next person enough context to maintain it.
