Direction: Type and nothing else, seed 1586040213. The name becomes the large dot-matrix identity; Jordan's browser experiments become the unequal product wall. The work is the live thing: linked interactive tools plus the real fractal recording with a play/pause control.

Two section redraws: the 10–18-name work paragraph was unsuitable for four visual projects, so use an unequal wall; the single-field contact blueprint implied an unavailable backend, so use a direct display email. Compact footer and contact are combined in purpose rather than duplicated as two full-screen endings.

The requested Nothing inspiration takes precedence over a generic direction's saturated ground and two-colour headline. Off-white/black fields, signal-red actions and locally hosted Doto/Public Sans give the site its direction without using Nothing logos, proprietary fonts, product images or marketing copy. Doto is an intentional dot-matrix brand exception to the inspector's monospace prohibition. All ordinary text uses Public Sans.
